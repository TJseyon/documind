New Hire Onboarding FAQ

Q: When do I get my laptop?
A: IT ships your laptop to arrive by your start date. If it hasn't arrived
two business days before you start, contact it-support@example.com.

Q: How do I set up payroll?
A: Payroll setup happens automatically from the information you submit in
Workday during your first week. Direct deposit typically activates within
one pay cycle.

Q: Who is my point of contact for benefits questions?
A: Contact HR at hr@example.com or through the internal HR portal. HR
handles health insurance enrollment, 401k enrollment, and any benefits
questions.

Q: What is the dress code?
A: Business casual for office days. Fully remote employees have no dress
code requirement.

Q: How many monitors can I request for my home office?
A: Employees may request up to two external monitors through the IT
equipment request form.
